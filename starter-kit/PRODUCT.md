# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

FlexShop developers use this starter kit to create single-product sales pages for shoppers in Nigeria. Visitors often arrive from campaign links on mobile devices and need to understand the product, offer, delivery terms, and next action quickly.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reusable React foundation for product-specific sales pages that are clear, fast, and ready to connect to the approved FlexShop ERP. It is a sales-page kit, not a storefront or payment platform.

## Brand Personality

Direct, reassuring, and practical. Pages should feel product-led and easy to act on without overstating the product or manufacturing pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS layouts, luxury minimalism that hides product information, cluttered storefront navigation, misleading countdowns, fabricated testimonials, unsupported claims, and inaccessible low-contrast campaign colors.

## Design Principles

1. Make the product and its practical benefit clear before asking for an order.
2. Let approved product imagery and information carry the sales argument.
3. Keep each page focused on one product, its approved offers, and one clear order path.
4. Use bold campaign colors deliberately while maintaining readable contrast.
5. Treat customer trust as a feature: show only verified facts, approved proof, and clear next steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard-access requirements, preserve visible focus states, support reduced motion, use semantic landmarks and labels, and design mobile-first for a broad range of reading and device conditions.
